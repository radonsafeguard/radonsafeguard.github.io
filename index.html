Why It Matters

Radon Is the #2 Cause of Lung Cancer

Radon is an invisible, odorless radioactive gas that seeps into buildings through foundation cracks and gaps. Health Canada estimates radon exposure causes roughly 16% of lung cancer deaths in Canada. Testing is the only way to know your risk.

1 in 15 Homes Affected

Alberta homes are among the most at risk in Canada - many Edmonton-area properties exceed Health Canada's guideline of 200 Bq/m3.

Common Questions About Radon

Quick answers to help you understand radon risks, testing, and mitigation.

Radon is a colorless, odorless radioactive gas that seeps into buildings from the ground. Health Canada identifies it as the leading cause of lung cancer in non-smokers — testing is the only way to know if your property is affected.

Our standard short-term test runs 48 hours using a continuous radon monitor. Results are delivered within 24 hours of test completion — fast enough for real estate closings.

Health Canada recommends taking action when radon levels reach 200 becquerels per cubic metre (Bq/m³) or higher. Many Edmonton homeowners also choose to mitigate at lower levels for added safety.

A fan and PVC piping system draws radon from beneath your foundation and vents it safely above the roofline. Most systems reduce radon levels by 80–99% and operate quietly in the background.

Every job is priced based on the complexity of your specific property — including foundation type, building size, radon levels, and the mitigation system design required. Contact us for a free, no-obligation quote tailored to your situation.
