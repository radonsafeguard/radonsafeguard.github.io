Health Risks of Radon

Radon is the second leading cause of lung cancer in Canada. Learn why mitigation is essential for protecting your health and your family's future.

Health Impact

The Silent Killer in Our Homes

Radon is a colorless, odorless, radioactive gas that seeps into buildings from the ground. It is the #1 cause of lung cancer among non-smokers and is responsible for thousands of preventable deaths every year in Canada.

Because you can't see, smell, or taste radon, many families are unknowingly exposed to dangerous levels for years. The good news is that the risk is entirely preventable with proper testing and mitigation.

The Facts

Why Radon Mitigation Saves Lives

Understanding the health science behind radon exposure makes it clear why professional mitigation is one of the most important steps you can take for your home.

Leading Cause of Lung Cancer in Non-Smokers

Health Canada estimates that radon causes about 16% of lung cancer deaths in Canada — more than second-hand smoke, asbestos, and air pollution combined.

Long-Term Exposure is Extremely Dangerous

Even moderate levels of radon (above 200 Bq/m³) significantly increase lifetime cancer risk. The risk rises dramatically the longer you are exposed and the higher the concentration.

Children and Smokers Face Higher Risks

Children are more susceptible because their lungs are still developing. Smokers exposed to radon have a risk of lung cancer that is 10 to 25 times higher than non-smokers.

Mitigation Dramatically Reduces Risk

A properly installed radon mitigation system can reduce radon levels by 80–99%, bringing them well below Health Canada’s guideline and dramatically lowering your long-term health risk.
