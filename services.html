Mitigation

Radon Mitigation Systems

When results are elevated relative to recommended action levels (or when you want to reduce exposure further), a professionally designed mitigation system is the proven approach. We install active soil depressurization (ASD) systems tailored to foundation type, layout, and measured levels.

Every installation includes a post-mitigation test to confirm levels have been reduced as much as practical — backed by our 5-year workmanship warranty. No level of radon is risk-free, so lower is always better.

  • Sub-slab depressurization for basements & slab-on-grade
  • Crawl space sub-membrane depressurization
  • Low-profile, quiet fan systems with visual gauges

Maintenance

Maintenance of Existing Systems

Even the best-designed mitigation systems need ongoing care. As buildings settle, materials age, and new cracks form, systems can gradually lose efficiency — allowing radon levels to creep back up over the years.

Our maintenance programs keep your system at peak performance, help hold radon as low as practical over time, extend equipment life, and protect your investment in healthier indoor air. We can also help with piping upgrades, noise reduction, smart monitoring, and post-service verification when you need it.

  • Annual full system inspections and performance testing
  • Fan and motor replacement
  • Re-sealing cracks, joints, and penetrations
  • Piping inspection, clearing, and component upgrades

How It Works

Radon Mitigation Explained

Active soil depressurization is the most effective method for reducing radon in Alberta homes.

1. Radon Enters

Radon gas rises from uranium in soil and seeps through your foundation, cracks, and utility gaps.

2. System Installed

A small hole is drilled in the foundation slab and connected to PVC piping routed to a quiet fan.

3. Fan Draws Gas

The fan creates negative pressure beneath the slab, pulling radon from the soil before it enters your home.

4. Safe Air

Radon is vented to the home's exterior and dissipates harmlessly. Post-mitigation testing confirms success.
